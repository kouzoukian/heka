---
title: Tray
---

Unless you choose otherwise in [App settings](/docs/using/app-settings), closing the window
leaves heka running in the notification area, with every Bridge. Click the tray icon to
open the window again. Right-click it for the list of Controllers with their state and
battery, **Open heka**, **Settings**, **Start with Windows** and **Quit**. Quitting removes
the Virtual Controllers and restores the Hidden Controllers.
